# PyTorch language-model experiments

**Timing:** Weeks 17–22

## Scope

Train count-based and neural bigram models, then a context-window MLP. Explore initialization, activation statistics and manual backprop.

## Acceptance gates

- [ ] Train/dev/test negative log likelihood
- [ ] At least two simple baselines
- [ ] One manual-backprop verification against autograd
- [ ] Activation/gradient diagnostic plots
- [ ] Two learning-rate or context-window ablations

```text
README.md    — this specification plus your design decisions
src/         — your own algorithms (create when you start)
tests/       — correctness checks (create when you start)
reports/     — reproducible experiment notes (create when you start)
```

## Before seeing reference solutions

Make a hypothesis, write a minimal prototype, add tests and compare with mathematical/numerical ground truth. Note failure modes and hardware limits.
